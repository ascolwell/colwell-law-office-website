---
question: "What's the difference between a will and a living trust?"
order: 2
---

A will tells the court how you'd like your property distributed — but assets passed through a will still generally go through Wisconsin probate court, a process that's public, and can take many months.

A revocable living trust is designed to let your family skip probate entirely for assets held in the trust. You keep full control during your lifetime, and you can change it anytime. Most families who want to make things as simple as possible for their loved ones choose a trust-based plan.

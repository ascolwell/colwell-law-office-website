---
question: "Do I really need an estate plan, or is that just for wealthy people?"
order: 1
---

Estate planning isn't about how much money you have — it's about the people who depend on you. If you own a home, have savings or retirement accounts, or have a spouse or children who count on you, you have something worth protecting.

Most of our clients are everyday Wisconsin families, not the ultra-wealthy. The real question isn't "am I rich enough?" — it's "do I want my family to have clear answers, or be left guessing?"

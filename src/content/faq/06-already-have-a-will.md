---
question: "I already have a will from years ago — is that a problem?"
order: 6
---

It's common, and it's fixable. Life changes — a move, a marriage, a new child, a new home — and an old will may no longer reflect what you actually want, or may not take full advantage of tools like a trust to avoid probate. We can review what you already have and build from there; you don't need to start from scratch.
